Privacy Policy for Bento Shop

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Bento Shop (the "Game") is a mobile game published under the Quadsphere Campus label by Rekiaro Entertainment Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"), the company responsible for the Game and the data controller for the purposes of this policy. This policy explains what information is involved when you play it.

The short version: We do not ask for your name, your email, or any account, and we do not collect anything about you ourselves. The Game shows ads to stay free, and our advertising partners collect device data in order to do that. You choose whether those ads may be personalised, and you can change your mind at any time.

Information we collect

None. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. Your progress, settings, unlocked items, and language choice are saved only in the Game's own storage on your device. We never receive them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

The Game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, contains no in-app purchases, and does not read your contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone, or camera.

Advertising and Mediation

The Game shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay mediation (operated by Unity Technologies). LevelPlay mediates ads from our advertising partners:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Ads)
  • Google LLC (Google AdMob)

To select ads, measure whether they were seen, and detect ad fraud, these partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (e.g. Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it (country or region level)
  • device information such as model, operating system version, carrier, and language
  • information about the ads themselves — which were shown, viewed, skipped, or tapped
  • diagnostic and fraud-prevention telemetry

Our advertising partners collect and process this information as independent controllers under their respective privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google LLC: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do not receive, see, or store any of this device data. We only receive aggregate, non-personal totals of how many ads were displayed.

Your choice about personalised ads

The first time you launch the Game, it asks whether ads may be personalised using your device's advertising identifier:

  • Yes — our advertising partners may use your advertising identifier to choose ads they think are relevant to you.
  • No thanks — you see contextual ads instead, chosen without your advertising identifier.

Either way the Game plays exactly the same, and either way ads still appear. Your answer is stored on your device and applied to every ad request, including the very first one after launch. Because it is a lasting privacy decision rather than game progress, it is deliberately kept even if you reset your progress in Settings.

Changing your answer

You can change your mind at any time in either of these ways:

  • Turn off ad personalisation for all apps on your device in system Settings → Google → Ads, using "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads personalisation". Our advertising partners honour this system setting and will serve only contextual ads.
  • Uninstall and reinstall the Game. This clears the stored answer, and the Game asks again on the next launch. It also erases your saved progress.

You can also email us at the address below and we will help.

Legal bases for processing (GDPR — players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Personalised ads: your consent, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, given through the popup described above. You may withdraw it at any time.
  • Contextual ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in funding a free game and keeping ad delivery secure and non-fraudulent.

Because data is collected by our advertising partners, they may transfer and process it outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their privacy policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to receive a copy of it.

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, or delete. All advertising data is held by our partners. You can exercise your rights directly through their respective privacy portals:

  • Unity Privacy Portal
  • Google Privacy Portal

If you would rather not deal with them directly, email us and we will pass your request on.

California residents

We do not sell personal information for money. Showing personalised ads based on your advertising identifier may count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. Choosing "No thanks" in the Game's popup, or turning off ad personalisation in your device settings, opts you out of that sharing. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Where a player is identified as a child under applicable law, or where the Game is distributed to a child audience, only non-personalised ads are served. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, please contact us and we will help get it removed.

Data retention

We retain nothing, because we collect nothing. Data held by our advertising partners is retained according to the periods set out in their privacy policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we add anything that changes how data is handled — for example leaderboards, cloud saves, in-app purchases, or analytics — we will update this policy and the effective date at the top before the change goes live.
